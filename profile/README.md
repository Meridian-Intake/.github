# Meridian Intake

**Private intake infrastructure for high-value service businesses.**

Meridian helps service operators recover missed calls, qualify inbound demand, route conversations, and produce inspectable intake evidence. We focus on businesses where missed inbound demand is expensive, time-sensitive, and operationally messy, including law firms, clinics, dental practices, and premium home-service businesses.

Meridian is not a generic chatbot company, AI agency, or call-centre replacement. We build controlled, reviewable intake systems that combine approved workflows, measurable recovery, clear escalation, and human oversight.

---

## What Meridian Does

Meridian operates a controlled intake layer that helps businesses:

- Detect missed or delayed inbound calls.
- Send approved SMS responses.
- Qualify demand using structured questions.
- Route conversations to the right person or team.
- Log intake activity for review and accountability.
- Produce reporting on missed-call recovery and intake performance.
- Maintain evidence for QA, procurement, and operating reviews.

## Who We Serve

### Law Firms

Recover missed calls, qualify potential matters, route inquiries, and maintain intake evidence without giving legal advice.

### Clinics and Medical Aesthetics

Respond to missed appointment inquiries, qualify interest, and route requests while avoiding diagnosis, treatment recommendations, or medical triage.

### Dental and Implant Practices

Recover high-value patient inquiries, qualify appointment intent, and escalate conversations to the right team.

### Premium Home Services

Capture missed inbound demand, qualify project fit, and route urgent or high-value opportunities.

## Operating Principles

1. **Control before automation** — Intake systems should be supervised, bounded, and reviewable before they scale.
2. **Professional boundaries matter** — Meridian does not provide legal advice, medical advice, diagnosis, triage, or regulated professional judgment.
3. **Evidence beats hype** — Every deployment should produce inspectable logs, routing records, QA notes, and performance reporting.
4. **Privacy is operational, not decorative** — Intake systems should minimize unnecessary data collection and protect sensitive client information.
5. **Revenue recovery must be measurable** — Missed-call recovery, response speed, qualification quality, and escalation performance should be tracked clearly.

## What Meridian Does Not Do

Meridian does **not**:

- Give legal advice.
- Give medical advice.
- Diagnose, triage, or recommend treatment.
- Replace licensed professionals.
- Make final client acceptance decisions.
- Operate as an unsupervised black-box chatbot.
- Store secrets or sensitive client data in public repositories.

## Repository Standards

Meridian repositories follow a controlled operating standard:

- Private-by-default for production systems.
- Protected `main` branches.
- Pull-request-based changes.
- No secrets committed to code.
- No client transcripts or sensitive intake data committed to GitHub.
- Environment variables managed through secure secret stores.
- Clear separation between development, staging, and production.
- Documentation maintained alongside system changes.
