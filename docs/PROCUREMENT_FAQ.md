# Procurement FAQ

## What does Meridian provide?

Meridian provides controlled intake infrastructure for high-value service businesses. It helps detect missed inbound demand, send approved follow-up messages, qualify requests with structured questions, route conversations, and produce reviewable intake evidence.

## Is Meridian a chatbot agency or call centre replacement?

No. Meridian is positioned as an intake-control layer with bounded automation, structured workflows, and human oversight. It does not replace licensed professionals or final acceptance decisions.

## Does Meridian provide legal or medical advice?

No. Meridian does not provide legal advice, medical advice, diagnosis, treatment recommendations, triage, or regulated professional judgment.

## What data should be kept out of public repositories?

Public repositories must not include secrets, credentials, client records, patient data, transcripts, voicemails, recordings, privileged information, or confidential operating details.

## How does Meridian support reviewability?

Meridian emphasizes logs, routing records, QA notes, reporting definitions, approved scripts, escalation rules, and performance reviews so intake activity can be inspected and improved.

## How should examples be shared publicly?

Use synthetic data only. Public examples should demonstrate structure and controls without revealing real clients, callers, conversations, or internal implementation details.
