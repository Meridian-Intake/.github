# Code of Conduct

Meridian expects professional, respectful, and privacy-conscious participation in public repositories.

## Expected Behavior

- Communicate clearly and respectfully.
- Protect confidential and sensitive information.
- Keep discussions focused on repository artifacts and operational outcomes.
- Respect legal, medical, privacy, and professional boundaries.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Sharing secrets, credentials, client records, patient data, transcripts, or privileged information.
- Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, or personal attacks.
- Attempts to bypass security, privacy, or review processes.
- Publishing unverified claims about regulated outcomes.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, close, or remove content that violates these expectations and may restrict participation when needed to protect people, clients, or systems.
